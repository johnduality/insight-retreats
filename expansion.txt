INSIGHT RETREATS — EXPANSION PLAN
=================================

Add one region at a time: discover -> research -> ingest -> verify, then move on.

SCOPE (set 2026-07-16, revised same day): this plan covers the United States (done,
pre-existing catalogue), Europe, Canada, Oceania, and Mexico — that is the full extent of
the expansion. Mexico is the only Latin American country in scope (added by request);
the rest of Latin America and other regions remain explicitly out of scope.
