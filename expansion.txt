INSIGHT RETREATS — EXPANSION PLAN
=================================

Add one region at a time: discover -> research -> ingest -> verify, then move on.

SCOPE (set 2026-07-16, revised three times same day): this plan covers the United
States (done, pre-existing catalogue), Europe, Canada, Oceania, all of Latin America,
and the non-state US territories — that is the full extent of the expansion. Mexico
was the first Latin American country in scope (added by request, now DONE — see
MEXICO SECTION COMPLETE below); the rest of Latin America was added to scope by
request on 2026-07-16 and is planned in the LATIN AMERICA (REST OF) section below.
The non-state US territories were added to scope by request the same day and are
planned in the US TERRITORIES section below (Russia was floated and then withdrawn
the same day — not in scope). Other regions (Africa, the Middle East, South/Southeast
Asia, etc.) remain explicitly out of scope.
