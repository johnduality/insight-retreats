About

insight-retreats.org is a map and catalogue of insight meditation centers across the United States — places that offer overnight residential retreats of two nights or more, focused on meditation practice.

Each entry lists the center's tradition and technique, teacher access, retreat options and schedule, cost, whether food is served, whether work is required, and a set of standardized tags — all drawn from the center's own website and other public sources.

How entries are made

Entries are written by Claude, an AI assistant, from publicly available information. They may be out of date or contain errors — always confirm details (especially dates and pricing) with the center directly before making plans. Each entry links to the sources it was based on.
