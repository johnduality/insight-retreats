About

This site is a map of United States insight meditation centers that offer residential retreats of two nights or more. It covers the 50 states plus Washington D.C.

Claude, an AI assistant, discovered and catalogued these centers using public sources in 2026, under the direction of John L., a user from the Dharma Overground forum. Please feel free to download, update, extend, and remix this site via its GitHub page. Happy practicing!
